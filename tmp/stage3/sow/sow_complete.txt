1. Introduction
1.1 Purpose
This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) document defines the complete functional, technical,
and business requirements for Amora AI — a premium AI-powered dating and matchmaking platform
developed by The Data Sequence. This document serves as the authoritative reference for all
stakeholders including investors, the development team, product managers, QA engineers, and
operational leads throughout the entire software development lifecycle.
The SRS establishes a shared understanding of the system's scope, capabilities, user roles, technical
architecture, revenue model, and delivery milestones. It is engineered to eliminate ambiguity, provide
measurable acceptance criteria, and ensure alignment between the founding vision and technical
execution.
Amora AI is not simply a dating application. It is a full-stack relationship technology ecosystem
combining AI-powered matchmaking, verified identity, offline event experiences, in-app commerce, and
a multi-stream monetization engine — all engineered for Day 1 profitability.
1.2 Vision
"Amora AI is not just a dating application. It is a complete premium matchmaking
ecosystem connecting verified singles through AI-driven compatibility, curated offline
experiences, and community-first relationship intelligence — built for Gujarat, designed for
India."
Amora AI envisions a future where finding a meaningful relationship is as seamless, safe, and
personalized as any premium digital experience. By connecting verified singles through AI compatibility
science, curated events, and intelligent conversation tools, Amora AI aims to become the most trusted
relationship platform in India.
Inspired by the monetization precision of Tinder, the community depth of Bumble, the AI intelligence of
Hinge, and the event ecosystem of exclusive social clubs, Amora AI is purpose-built for the Indian Tier-
2 premium market — starting with Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar, and Vadodara.

3. User Roles
The Amora AI platform operates on a tri-role architecture. Each role has distinct permissions,
workflows, dashboards, and access controls engineered for their specific journey.
3.1 Customer (App User)
The Customer is the primary end-user of the platform — an individual aged 18–35 seeking genuine
romantic connections, curated dating experiences, and AI-assisted relationship guidance.
Customer Capabilities:
• Register and create a verified profile with identity verification (Aadhaar-linked selfie or
government ID)
• Complete AI-powered compatibility onboarding questionnaire (40+ behavioral and preference
signals)
• Discover potential matches through AI Compatibility Feed, Smart Filters, and Curated Daily Picks
• Swipe, Like, Super Like, and send Rose signals to potential matches
• Initiate and conduct conversations via in-app messaging with AI icebreaker suggestions
• Subscribe to Starter (₹699), Plus (₹1,199), Gold (₹1,999), or VIP (₹3,499) monthly plans
• Purchase in-app microtransactions: Boosts, Super Likes, Profile Spotlights, Priority Visibility,
Direct Messages
• Access AI Dating Coach for personalized daily tips, conversation scoring, and relationship
guidance
• Discover, register for, and attend offline events: Speed Dating, Singles Mixers, Premium Date
Nights
• Send virtual and physical gifts to matches via in-app gifting commerce
• Earn and redeem loyalty points through Amora Coins for every interaction, trip, and referral
• Refer friends via unique referral code and earn wallet credits upon successful first subscription
• Report profiles, block users, and access 24/7 AI-assisted and WhatsApp customer support
3.2 Host (Event / Brand Partner)
The Host is an event organizer, local business, or brand partner who registers on the platform to co-
create offline experiences, sponsored content, and partnership revenue with Amora AI.
Host Capabilities:
• Register as a verified Host with business credentials and GST/PAN documentation
• Submit offline event proposals (Speed Dating, Mixers, Rooftop Nights, Coffee Meetups) for
Admin approval
• Set event details: venue, ticket pricing, capacity, date, category, and target audience
• Purchase sponsored profile placements, native feed ads, and push notification campaigns
• Access co-branding packages for Brand Sponsorship at ₹50K–₹2L per event
• List venue or business as a 'Date Spot' on the Amora in-app map for ₹5K–₹15K/month
• Monitor event bookings, ticket sales, and attendee confirmations via Host Dashboard
• View event performance analytics: attendance, engagement, and post-event match conversion
• Communicate with the Amora events team via in-app messaging and WhatsApp Business

4. Core Modules
4.1 Authentication Module
The Authentication Module provides secure, multi-channel identity management for all three platform
roles. It supports mobile OTP-based login (preferred for Indian users), email/password authentication,
and social sign-in via Google. Features include JWT token-based session management, biometric
authentication (fingerprint/face unlock) on supported devices, device binding for fraud prevention,
automatic session refresh with configurable expiry, and role-based access control (RBAC) that redirects
users to their respective dashboards upon authentication.
4.2 Match Discovery Module
The Match Discovery Module is the core matchmaking engine of the platform. Users encounter
potential matches through four discovery surfaces: the AI Compatibility Feed (personalized daily stack
of 10–30 profiles), the Browse Grid (city-wide browsable gallery), the Curated Picks (3–5 AI-selected
high-compatibility profiles per day on Gold/VIP), and the Event Attendees list (matched users attending
the same upcoming event).
Discovery filters include age range, city, education, profession, relationship intent, language,
community affiliation, and AI compatibility score threshold. Smart sorting surfaces high-engagement
profiles during peak usage windows (7–10 PM) to maximize match conversion rates.
4.3 Booking & Event Module
The Booking & Event Module orchestrates the complete offline event lifecycle from discovery to post-
event match conversion. Users browse upcoming events by category (Speed Dating, Mixers,
Professional Events, VIP Nights), register with one-tap booking, and receive digital event passes.
The module enforces event capacity limits, waitlist management, ticket transfer policies, and
cancellation fee enforcement. All attendee data is shared with the Host dashboard in real time. Post-
event, the platform automatically surfaces 'You Both Attended' match suggestions to drive digital-to-
physical connection.
4.4 Subscription & Payment Module
The Subscription & Payment Module integrates with Razorpay to provide a comprehensive, PCI-
compliant subscription and payment experience. Five subscription tiers are supported: Free (₹0),
Starter (₹699/mo), Plus (₹1,199/mo), Gold (₹1,999/mo), and VIP (₹3,499/mo). Annual subscription
options provide a 2-month discount incentive.
Payment methods include UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking, Debit/Credit Cards, and Amora
Wallet balance. Features include auto-renewal with 3-day pre-notification, payment failure recovery with
retry logic, proration on mid-cycle upgrades, and GST-compliant invoicing. Security deposit
authorization is used for premium event bookings.

4.5 AI Matchmaking Engine
The AI Matchmaking Engine is Amora's core proprietary technology. It computes a bidirectional
Compatibility Score (0–100) for every potential user pair using 40+ behavioral and preference signals
collected during onboarding and ongoing platform activity.
Scoring dimensions include: Personality Archetype (MBTI-adjacent), Relationship Intent Alignment,
Lifestyle Compatibility (diet, fitness, social habits), Professional & Educational Context, Communication
Style, Cultural & Community Alignment, and Behavioral Signals (response rate, message quality, swipe
patterns). The engine retrains on Gujarat-specific interaction data every 72 hours, continuously
improving local match accuracy.
4.6 In-App Messaging Module
The In-App Messaging Module enables secure, feature-rich communication between matched users.
Core chat features include text messaging, emoji reactions, photo sharing (with AI moderation), audio
messages, and video call scheduling (Gold/VIP).
The AI Icebreaker Engine generates 3 personalized conversation starter suggestions for every new
match based on shared interests, compatibility dimensions, and the match's recent activity. Read
receipts are available as a microtransaction feature. Message delivery uses WebSocket (Firebase
Realtime Database) for sub-500ms latency.
4.7 AI Dating Coach Module
The AI Dating Coach is a premium subscription add-on (₹599/mo) that serves as a personal
relationship intelligence assistant. The Coach provides daily personalized tips, conversation quality
scoring (after user opts in to share chat excerpts), date idea suggestions based on the match's verified
interests, and post-date reflection prompts.
The AI Relationship Advisor (₹799/mo) extends the Coach with long-form relationship analysis,
attachment style assessment, communication pattern feedback, and conflict resolution guidance. The
AI Date Planner (₹249/session) generates location-specific date itineraries for confirmed matches,
including venue recommendations, activity suggestions, and conversation topic prompts.
4.8 Events & Experiences Module
The Events & Experiences Module is a primary revenue driver and Amora's key competitive
differentiator. It manages the complete lifecycle of offline events across all three launch cities. Event
types include Speed Dating Nights (₹799–₹1,299/ticket), Singles Mixers (₹999–₹1,999), Verified Match
Coffee (₹399/person), Professional Mixers (₹1,499–₹2,499), Premium Date Nights
(₹2,499–₹4,999/couple), and VIP Experiences.
The module handles venue partner management, brand sponsorship configuration (₹50K–₹2L per
event), ticket pre-sale with 60%+ pre-sell targets, event check-in with QR code validation, and post-
event analytics. Events run at 65% gross margin and generate 200+ organic social posts per event,
driving free app installs.

4.9 Profile & Verification Module
The Profile & Verification Module manages user identity, document verification, and the Blue-Tick
verified profile system that forms Amora's trust foundation. Verification uses Aadhaar-linked selfie
verification or government ID photo match via third-party OCR API.
Profile features include multi-photo upload (up to 9 photos with AI quality scoring), audio bio recording
(30 seconds), video introduction (60 seconds, Gold/VIP), interest tags (50+ pre-defined categories),
relationship intent declaration, and prompt-based profile questions. Verified profiles receive a Blue-Tick
badge that increases match rate by an estimated 3.2× based on comparable platform data.
4.10 Referral & Rewards Module
The Referral & Rewards Module drives organic user acquisition through a structured incentive system.
Each registered user receives a unique referral code. Referrer earns ₹100 Amora Coins when a
referred user completes their first paid subscription. Referred user receives a ₹150 first-subscription
discount. K-factor target of 1.2 ensures each user drives more than one new registration organically.
The Amora Coins loyalty system awards points for every completed action: first match (50 coins), first
message sent (25 coins), event attendance (200 coins), profile completion (100 coins), and streak
bookings. Coins are redeemable for in-app features, event ticket discounts, and AI pack trials.
4.11 In-App Gifting & Commerce Module
The In-App Gifting & Commerce Module enables users to express interest and affection through a
curated marketplace of virtual and physical gifts. Virtual gifts include Roses (₹99), Star Bundles (₹199),
and Chocolate Boxes (₹149). Physical gift delivery is fulfilled via partner logistics at ₹299–₹1,499, with
15% platform commission.
The Date Package Commerce feature offers curated date experiences: restaurant + activity + gift
bundles at ₹999–₹3,999. Partner restaurants pay ₹2K–₹5K for featured listing in the Date Spots
section. The platform earns 10–15% commission on all commerce transactions, contributing an
estimated ₹1.5L/month in Month 1.
4.12 Support System Module
The Support System Module provides multi-channel customer assistance through WhatsApp Business
API integration, in-app AI chatbot for common queries, and escalation pathways to human support
agents for safety and payment issues.
Support features include contextual help triggered from subscription, event, and matching screens;
structured FAQ categories covering matching, payments, events, AI features, and safety; WhatsApp
chat initiation with pre-filled account context; incident reporting with photo evidence upload for profile
abuse; and SLA-based ticket tracking with 2-hour first response guarantee for P1 issues.

5. Advanced Features
5.1 AI Compatibility Scoring Engine
Every pair of users on the platform receives a live Compatibility Score (0–100%) computed from 40+
behavioral and preference signals. Scores update every 72 hours based on new activity data. The
engine is trained on Gujarat-specific interaction patterns, improving match quality progressively as the
dataset grows. Users can view their compatibility breakdown with any match on Gold and VIP tiers.
5.2 Dynamic Pricing & Yield Management
Similar to airline yield management, the platform supports demand-based dynamic ticket pricing for
events. During high-demand periods (Valentine's Day, Navratri, New Year's Eve), event ticket prices
automatically adjust upward within host-defined bands. Subscription pricing remains fixed; however,
promotional pricing windows are managed through the Admin CMS without app updates.
5.3 Smart Match Availability Calendar
Users can set an 'Available to Meet' status window on their calendar, signaling to matches that they are
open to offline meetups in specific time blocks. The platform surfaces 'Available Now' signals on the
match feed, increasing real-world meeting conversion rates. Hosts can use this data to predict event
demand and optimize event scheduling.
5.4 In-App Identity Verification (Blue Tick)
KYC verification is embedded within the onboarding flow using Aadhaar selfie match or driving license
OCR parsing via third-party API (Digilocker / Surepass). Blue-Tick verified users receive a prominent
badge on their profile card, increased visibility in the discovery feed, and access to Verified Match
Coffee events — a premium verified-only event category.
5.5 AI Photo Moderation & Quality Scoring
All uploaded profile photos pass through an AI moderation pipeline that detects inappropriate content,
ensures photo clarity and face visibility, scores photo quality (lighting, composition, smile index), and
rejects non-compliant images with user-friendly feedback. This maintains the premium visual quality
standard of the platform and reduces manual moderation overhead by an estimated 70%.
5.6 AI Icebreaker Engine
For every new match, the AI Icebreaker Engine generates 3 personalized conversation starter options
based on shared interests extracted from both users' profiles, recent activity, and compatibility
dimensions. Users can tap one-touch to send the AI-generated opener or customize it. The Icebreaker
Pack (₹199/month as a standalone microtransaction) provides unlimited AI openers throughout the
month.

5.7 Real-Time Push Notifications
Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) powers a comprehensive notification framework covering: new
match alerts, message received, Super Like received, event registration confirmation, event reminder
(24h and 2h prior), subscription renewal notice, Amora Coin credit, referral reward, AI Coach daily tip,
and promotional offers. Notification preferences are fully configurable per category.
5.8 Offline Mode & Cached Views
Core screens including My Matches, Chat History, Upcoming Events, and Profile are cached locally
using Hive database, ensuring usability in low-connectivity environments common in Indian Tier-2
markets. Match cards are pre-loaded during active sessions so users can browse offline. Outgoing
messages queue locally and send automatically when connectivity is restored.
5.9 Multi-City Expansion Framework
The platform architecture supports seamless geographic expansion. City-specific configurations
including featured users, local event calendars, pricing zones, and operational partner lists are
managed through a centralized Admin CMS without requiring app updates. Adding a new city requires
only Admin configuration — no engineering work. Phase 2 targets Surat, Rajkot, Jamnagar, and
Bhavnagar within 6 months of launch.
5.10 Post-Event Match Conversion Engine
After every attended event, the platform automatically generates 'You Both Attended' match
suggestions within 24 hours, cross-referencing the attendee list with existing unmatched users on the
platform. Post-event match conversion rates of 2.8× the standard feed rate have been demonstrated in
pilot event data. This creates a compounding loop: events drive app engagement, app engagement
drives event ticket sales.

35–40% compared to traditional development approaches while maintaining enterprise-grade quality
standards and full code ownership.

• Product Planning: Generating comprehensive feature lists, user journey maps, and competitive

• Architecture Guidance: Evaluating Flutter architecture patterns for Amora's specific AI-heavy,

• UI Planning: Defining screen hierarchy, navigation patterns, and information architecture for each
user role
• Content Generation: Writing microcopy, AI Coach tips, icebreaker templates, onboarding content,
and FAQ responses
• Market Intelligence: Synthesizing Gujarat dating market sizing, event monetization benchmarks,
and competitor pricing strategies

• Flutter Widget Generation: Auto-completing complex widget trees for the swipe discovery feed,
match animation, AI icebreaker card, and event booking flow
• Reusable Component Creation: Generating the 55+ shared component library including custom
match cards, tier comparison matrix, event passes, and coach tip cards

• AI Integration Code: Generating OpenAI API client wrappers, response parsing, and error
handling patterns
• Test Generation: Auto-generating unit test scaffolding for repository classes and business logic
functions

partner:
