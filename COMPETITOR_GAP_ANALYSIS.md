# Competitor Gap Analysis

AMORA now matches premium dating-app expectations in visual hierarchy, discovery cards, verification cues, intentional matching language, premium upsell surfaces, and safety-forward UI.

Before backend launch, the largest gaps versus Tinder, Bumble, Hinge, Aisle, and Sangam are product capabilities rather than frontend presentation: live messaging, moderation workflows, payment entitlements, real verification, analytics, experimentation, and notification delivery.
